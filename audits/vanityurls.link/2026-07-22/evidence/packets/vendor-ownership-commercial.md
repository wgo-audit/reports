## Material Unknowns And Access Limits

- Current GitHub organization/repository owners, administrators, branch/tag rule administrators, and successor appointment authority are unknown.
- Current Cloudflare account/zone/Workers/Zero Trust owners and administrators, API-token custody, recovery factors, and transfer procedure are unknown.
- The short-domain registrar owner, renewal status, payment method, recovery contacts, and transfer authorization are unknown.
- Current live deployment, Terraform state custody, state back end, drift, monitoring, usage, quota, and incident ownership are unknown.
- Vendor plan, recurring spend, domain cost, analytics cost, other liabilities, funding, and any private commitments are unknown.
- These unknowns prevent a public-evidence-only conclusion that control of the existing public project and `v8s.link` instance can be transferred after a sudden departure. They do not prevent a third party from creating and operating a new independent instance from public source.

## Reuse Guidance

- Business Continuity and Security may reuse ownership and recovery gaps, but must not describe them as proven control failures.
- Contributor/Vendor Value and Project Health may reuse declared roles and concentration, but must not infer individual value or legal control from Git history.
- Expense Exposure and Revenue Risk may reuse the absence of approved commercial evidence only as an evidence limitation; dependencies are not cost facts.
- Architecture, Scalability, and Maintenance Cost may reuse vendor surfaces and Terraform declarations while keeping live state, ownership, cost, and effectiveness `unknown`.
