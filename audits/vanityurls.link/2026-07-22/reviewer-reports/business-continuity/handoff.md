# Business Continuity Handoff

## Confirmed Navigation

The [continuity/transfer matrix](../../controls/continuity/continuity-and-transfer-matrix.md) answers fork versus takeover; the [response path](../../controls/business-continuity/diagrams/observability-and-response-path.md) shows unproved detection, authority, and recovery handoffs.

## Constraints And Conflicts

Public source is portable, but canonical GitHub/domain/Cloudflare/release control is not transfer-proven. Documented rollback is not tested recovery.

## Material Unknowns

Owners, successor authority, state/secrets, renewals, alerts, on-call, RTO/RPO, deploy equivalence, and recovery results remain unknown.

## Downstream Use

Wave 3 may use continuity burdens and concentration. Do not infer outage, owner unavailability, spend, or absence of private arrangements.
