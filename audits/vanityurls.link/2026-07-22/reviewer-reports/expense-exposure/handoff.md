# Expense Exposure Handoff

## Confirmed Navigation

The [burn/renewal register](../../controls/expense/burn-and-renewal.md) records domain, GitHub, Cloudflare, website, analytics, labor, and incident exposure with every unsupported amount marked `unknown`.

## Constraints And Conflicts

Free-plan guidance is not entitlement or zero-cost proof. Dependencies are not invoices.

## Material Unknowns

Amounts, periods, plans, quotas, usage, renewals, payment owners, support, commitments, labor rates, and liabilities remain unknown.

## Downstream Use

Use interruption and verification needs only. Do not infer spend, revenue, contractual obligation, or zero cost.
