# Contributor And Vendor Value Handoff

## Confirmed Navigation

The [contribution value assessment](../../controls/contributors/contribution-value.md)
identifies feature-level learning paths; the
[ownership/successor map](../../controls/contributors/ownership-and-successor.md)
separately covers roles, vendors, and authority gaps.

## Constraints And Conflicts

Two contributors reach 95.3% of supported feature units, but attribution is not
performance, account control, or successor authority. Public history cannot
show hidden work, adoption, or live operation.

## Material Unknowns

Appointment authority, access, availability, competency coverage, support
entitlements, and exercised succession remain unknown.

## Downstream Use

Use CV-001–CV-009 to plan a non-creator handover. Do not use rankings for
compensation, blame, legal ownership, vendor performance, or customer value.
