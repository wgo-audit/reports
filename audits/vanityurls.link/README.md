## Conclusions over time

- vanityURLs has a portable codebase and identifiable public-evidence improvements; canonical continuity remains unresolved because it requires normally private transfer evidence that this audit could not assess.

| Question | 2026-07-22 |
|---|---|
| Can a new maintainer take over the canonical project with minimal creator involvement? | Not established from public evidence |
| Can a third party establish and operate a new, independently controlled vanityURLs instance? | Plausible, but not yet proven easy |
| Can a third party take over and operate the existing project, v8s.link domain, and demo service? | Unresolved; current-owner cooperation and private authority evidence are required |
| If the creators abandon the project, can someone else pick it up and evolve it? | Yes for a fork, probably with moderate effort; no assurance for canonical continuity |
