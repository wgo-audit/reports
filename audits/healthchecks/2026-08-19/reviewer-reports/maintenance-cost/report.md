# Maintenance Cost

## Audit Question, Depth, And Evidence Boundary

This detailed review asks what skill mix, initial and recurring opportunity-time burden, operational maintenance, hotspot exposure, and safe-change work Acme would accept under pull, make, or buy over 36 months. It uses the audit cutoff of 2026-08-19; the pinned `HC-CODE-001` source and Git history; completed Architecture, Code Quality, and Business Continuity evidence; relevant Contributor/Vendor evidence; and Acme's RTO 30 minutes, RPO 5 minutes, five-minute actionable-alert requirement, and cost-treatment decisions.

No Acme deployment, job inventory, selected topology/hosted plan, task timing, incident rate, maintenance automation, team capability, labor rate, vendor internals, or negotiated commitment was available. Acme directed that no application-architecture-change effort be assigned to pull or make, but deployment, hardening, integration, testing, recovery, upgrades, and operations remain in scope. Engineering time is opportunity cost and is not monetized.

## Coverage And Material Gaps

The review examined upstream production and Docker guidance, deployment/runtime/configuration surfaces, management and cleanup commands, the prior 36 months of source/release/path change history, large and high-churn Python surfaces, hosted public operating boundaries, upstream quality evidence, recovery/change controls, and ownership/succession evidence. It tested the preliminary 2-5-day setup and 4-8-hours/month claims rather than accepting them.

No responsible replacement hour or FTE range can be calculated. The missing activity durations, selected option design, automation state, job contracts, release uptake, incident demand, and skill coverage are material. [OI-018](../../controls/open-items.md#OI-018) requires a production-shaped pilot and measured opportunity-time ledger; [OI-019](../../controls/open-items.md#OI-019) requires an option-specific maintenance operating model. [OI-002](../../controls/open-items.md#OI-002) and [OI-016](../../controls/open-items.md#OI-016) retain team-coverage and successor proof without inferring current ability.

## Mandate-Relevant Strengths

- Upstream publishes versioned source and prebuilt images and supplies practical startup/upgrade guidance ([E-045](../../evidence/evidence-ledger.md#E-045), [E-046](../../evidence/evidence-ledger.md#E-046)); its hosted CI has a broad test matrix, strict typing, CodeQL runs, and reported 92% statement coverage at the pin ([E-009 through E-013](../../evidence/evidence-ledger.md#E-009)). This gives pull a credible base for an Acme-controlled promotion gate.
- The BSD-3-Clause license and complete source history preserve the right to continue, inspect, or modify the product if upstream availability changes ([E-029](../../evidence/evidence-ledger.md#E-029)). Rights do not prove successor capacity, but reduce legal source-access lock-in.
- The architecture is concentrated enough that the main state, transport, deployment, and recovery boundaries can be named and tested; the [hotspot view](../../controls/maintenance/hotspot-and-change-risk.md) turns that into a bounded change path instead of an undifferentiated “maintain the whole repository” claim.
- Buy materially reduces Acme's source, runtime, database, and platform patch workload, provided the remaining security, integration, independent-alert, account, recovery, and exit gates close.

### Decision Insights

1. **Do not use either preliminary effort number for approval or staffing.** The estimates omit the very controls that make the service safe for Acme's mandate. The wrong assumption risks both monitoring failure and hidden roadmap diversion. The smallest next proof is OI-018: measure a selected-option pilot by activity through one release/vendor-change cycle plus fault, recovery, capacity, and successor exercises.
2. **Keep make stopped unless a pull deployment proves a narrow source-level defect.** Make inherits every pull duty and adds a permanent fork/release organization across active central surfaces and concentrated upstream stewardship. The consequence of choosing it early is an unbounded 36-month opportunity-cost stream. OI-017's necessary-change and ownership gate is the smallest responsible decision point.
3. **Maintenance alone favors buy over self-hosting and pull over make, but does not approve either.** Buy removes the broadest runtime/source burden yet remains blocked on hosted security/continuity/exit proof. Pull retains more operating work but offers source/data control and lower source ownership than make. The smallest next action is to close the common job, alert, capacity, recovery, account, and successor gates, then measure the two viable candidates rather than fabricate hours.
4. **Budget routine and surge opportunity time separately.** Twenty-six upstream tags and clustered dependency/deployment changes over 36 months show why a flat monthly number is structurally weak, while incident/security/recovery work is stochastic. OI-019 should define event triggers and cadence; OI-018 should measure active time and surge separately.

## Reconciliation

No material source conflict was found. The intake explicitly labels its effort figures as preliminary claims, and the approved source supplies duties and change frequency but no task durations; the review therefore rejects the figures as validated planning baselines without asserting they are impossible. Acme's later RTO/RPO and cost-model decisions sharpen the acceptance and reporting boundary but do not prove current compliance. OI-002, OI-008, OI-016, and OI-017 remain distinct: capability comparison, promotion safety, successor coverage, and fork authorization are not replaced by OI-018/OI-019.

One attempted allocation of an already-used worker for artifact quality was interrupted immediately, produced no feedback or artifact state, and is not counted as the required independent quality review. The single fresh independent `gpt-5.6-terra`/high quality task `maintenance_cost_quality` then returned one terminal `REVISE` outcome: a P2 correction to separate the E-045/E-046 source-and-image claim from the E-009..E-013 hosted-quality claim in the first strengths bullet. The coordinator applied that exact citation correction as the one revision; all other quality checks passed. No quality task remains open.

## Bounded Conclusion And Downstream Guidance

The evidence establishes relative maintenance burden, not hours: pull has high initial and material event-driven recurring opportunity cost; make is no lower and remains unbounded without a necessary source change and fork charter; buy reduces Acme runtime/source maintenance but retains material security, integration, account, independent-alert, recovery/exit, and change-review work. Neither 2-5 setup days nor 4-8 hours/month is fit for planning without OI-018 measurement.

Project Health should use E-046 through E-050 and the selected controls to assess sustainability. It must not infer Acme team competence, convert relative bands to staffing/cash, assume the sample deployment is production-ready, treat hosted operation as maintenance-free, or approve make before OI-017.
