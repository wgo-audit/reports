### Unpopulated planning relationships

The following are **formulas, not forecasts or observed totals**:

- Vendor monthly list exposure = `($20 × Pro seats) + ($0.20 × production API/embed completions) + selected contract-confirmed metered charges`.
- Infrastructure run rate = `approved target resource quantities × organization rate cards`, including redundancy, backups, retained evidence, network/egress, monitoring, security tooling, and non-production environments.
- Staffing run rate = `approved role allocation × organization labor rates`; no role allocation or rate is in evidence.
- Signing unavailable-time planning ceiling = `calendar-month minutes × 0.005`; customer-onboarding unavailable-time planning ceiling = `calendar-month minutes × 0.01`. For a 30-day planning month these are 216 and 432 minutes respectively, not measured performance or a final SLO definition; measurement rules and exclusions remain to be approved.
- Recovery planning must meet a two-hour RPO, with synchronous transactions preferred. The target does not select a replication, backup, reconciliation, or failover design and therefore does not establish its resource cost or effectiveness.
- All new onboarding may pause during interruption, so the current target does not require pricing a degraded new-onboarding mode. EXP-OI-001 must still price response, recovery, vendor support, reconciliation, backlog/catch-up capacity, and evidence retention. Revenue loss and customer impact belong to Revenue Risk and are not calculated here.
