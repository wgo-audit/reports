- Status: observed
- Evidence cutoff: public pages observed 2026-08-06 as post-cutoff validation

## Decision Statement

DocuSeal publicly describes signatures as legally binding/compliant and audit evidence as independently verifiable, while the inspected Community source and approved evidence do not establish edition-specific legal, regulatory, KYC, or customer acceptance.

## Observed Position, Rationale, And Approval

| Dimension | Position | Evidence | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product behavior/promise | Broad ESIGN/UETA/eIDAS, integrity, identity, and audit assertions. | PV-E-005 | Dynamic, general, mixes services/editions. |
| Implementation | Conditional signing, snapshot audit, and bounded verifier semantics. | PV-E-004 | Cannot establish legal effect. |
| Runtime/demonstration | unknown | No artifact or assurance report | Applicability unproved. |
| Approval/specialist sign-off | unknown | Mandate reserves determinations | No authority acceptance. |

## Constraints, Options, And Tradeoffs

Public claims can guide vendor questions but cannot substitute for release-specific evidence, contract terms, or jurisdictional determinations.

## Impacts And Boundaries

Adopting these claims as audit conclusions would violate the mandate and could misstate readiness to executives and regulators.

## Change, Reversal, And Follow-Up

Request claim-to-edition/control/artifact mapping and assurance records; legal/compliance/CISO decide applicability after controlled artifact testing.
