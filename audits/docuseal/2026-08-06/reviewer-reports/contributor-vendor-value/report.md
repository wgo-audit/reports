## Audit Question, Depth, And Evidence Boundary

At detailed depth, this review asks what inspectable delivered output, feature-level contribution attribution, ownership, knowledge transfer, vendor dependency, and successor capability are evidenced for DocuSeal Community `3.1.7`, and what must remain unresolved before the organization attributes value or tolerates loss of a contributor, vendor, account, or service. The boundary is pinned source/history at `a2d8b855491793870b7b4acf176d2d95ae95ff83`, cutoff 2026-08-06 America/Toronto, E-001–E-006/E-011–E-018/E-025–E-029/E-044/E-046–E-053, the [feature contribution packet](../../evidence/packets/contributor-vendor-value-feature-contribution.md), [ownership/support packet](../../evidence/packets/contributor-vendor-value-ownership-support.md), and direct Product Value, Business Continuity, Expense, and shared GitHub evidence linked there. Later approved public pages remain `post-cutoff-validation`, not release-bound contracts or product proof.

Excluded are private `docusealco/wip` history/reviews, private repository settings, Pro implementation, hosted Cloud operation, vendor personnel/financial records, contracts, support cases, organization accounts/owners, time records, customer acceptance, and live transfer/replacement exercises. Git labels, emails, copyright, account actions, commits, PRs, merges, and lines changed are not employment, staffing, authority, effort, performance, support, solvency, or bus-factor evidence.

## Mandate-Relevant Strengths

- The Community core is source-available under AGPLv3 with repository-specific attribution terms and a fixed, traceable source pin rather than only an opaque hosted promise.
- The selected feature history supports material delivery across signing, PDF/evidence, API/webhooks, identity, queues, document authoring, security checks, mobile/disclosure affordances, and integrations.
- Successful declared CI and image-build workflows, frequent recent tags, direct source locators, and a public security-report route reduce several upstream-evaluation unknowns.
- Source-configurable infrastructure and providers expose concrete transfer and substitution points, allowing successor tests and vendor questions to be precise.

### Decision Insights

1. **Proceed-versus-stop:** substantive Community output and traceable delivery history support continued evaluation; stopping solely for lack of inspectable Pro code would discard evidenced core value. The next proof is target golden-path and artifact acceptance, not more activity counting.
2. **Sourcing decision:** Community code value, Pro feature value, and vendor support/commercial value are three different evidence classes. Bundling them could turn public features/prices into an unsupported contract claim; OI-001/OI-005/OI-020 must establish the selected edition/component/term matrix.
3. **Transfer decision:** absent successor/transfer proof across broad source, application, runtime, data, key, and provider duties creates the High consequence; the judgment-selected author-name-group attribution pattern is only a trigger to verify succession. A knowledge, person-dependency, or “bus factor” inference would be unsupported. The smallest valid proof is two organization maintainers independently building, patching, testing, deploying, recovering, and transferring the pinned service under OI-015/OI-016.
4. **Vendor-dependency decision:** visible cadence and a security mailbox do not establish support/patch commitments or accountable authority. OI-013 and OI-020 must bind supported versions, response/notification, escalation, renewal, continued operation, transition, and exit to operative evidence before production commitment.

## Bounded Conclusion And Downstream Guidance

DocuSeal Community `3.1.7` contains a material, inspectable delivered source foundation and a traceable feature-delivery history; it supports further technical and vendor evaluation. The evidence does not establish who holds upstream authority, how private work/review is governed, whether support or Pro value is contractually accepted, whether the vendor is healthy or replaceable, or whether the organization can transfer and sustain control. Contributor/Vendor Value therefore supports **continue evaluation conditionally**, not production approval, vendor acceptance, or a personnel conclusion.

Project Health may use the bounded feature units, annual/lifetime attribution distribution, governance surface, and vendor-dependency map. Maintenance Cost may use the successor/edition boundaries to scope replacement proof. Neither may infer employment, staffing, authority, performance, effort, support responsiveness, solvency, bus factor, contract acceptance, spend, or replacement cost from Git labels or weighted units.
