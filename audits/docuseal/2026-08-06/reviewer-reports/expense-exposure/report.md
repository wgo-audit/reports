# Expense Exposure

## Audit Question, Depth, And Evidence Boundary

At detailed depth, this review asks what actual or potential cash and interruption exposure arises from DocuSeal infrastructure, software, staffing, commitments, usage, and failure modes for regulated web/mobile onboarding. The cutoff is 2026-08-06. Evidence includes pinned Community `3.1.7` source at `a2d8b855491793870b7b4acf176d2d95ae95ff83`, E-001/E-005/E-013–E-018/E-025–E-043, the completed Architecture/Product Value/Security handoffs and linked direct artifacts/packets, the [expense commercial packet](../../evidence/packets/expense-exposure-vendor-ownership-commercial.md), and the auditor's 2026-08-06 answer setting continuity targets. Approved dynamic public pricing, terms, and sizing pages are used only as `post-cutoff-validation`.

Coordinator mapping: local EXP-OI-001/002/003 are serialized as canonical OI-018/OI-019/OI-020; expense evidence is consolidated as E-044/E-051–E-053.

Excluded: invoices, purchase orders, quotes, signed Pro contracts, billing/usage consoles, payment data, workload forecasts, target architecture, infrastructure rate cards, staffing/on-call records, live operation, Pro implementation, Cloud assessment, legal conclusions, and production approval. Source dependencies and open controls are cost drivers, not spend; public prices are list-price signals, not observed or committed cash.

## Coverage And Material Gaps

Coverage includes Community/Pro licensing; Pro seats; API/embed completion metering; optional SMS/identity/signature unit charges; public payment/cancellation signals; approximate resource dimensions; runtime/data/job/provider dependencies; security, recovery, promotion and observability work; staffing/ownership; billing/renewal/interruption boundaries; and the confirmed 99.5% monthly signing availability, 99% monthly customer-onboarding availability, two-hour RPO with synchronous transactions preferred, and permission to pause all new onboarding during interruption.

No approved evidence establishes actual spend, budget, committed spend, target volume, negotiated price, term/renewal, discounts, taxes/currency, chosen infrastructure, redundancy/retention, staffing rates, billing owner, quota controls, or self-hosted Pro behavior after payment failure/cancellation. Actual and total potential cost therefore cannot be bounded. Canonical OI-018–OI-020 route the workload/total-cost proof, commercial-owner decision, and vendor quote/agreement proof.

## Mandate-Relevant Strengths

- Public on-premises pricing exposes seat, completion, and selected identity/signature units clearly enough to define an unpopulated planning formula and a precise vendor evidence request.
- The pinned source and dependency artifacts identify the runtime, store, job, delivery, trust, security and lifecycle surfaces that a target cost model must include.
- Community and Pro boundaries are explicit enough to keep the zero-license Community path, Pro list-price path, and organization-owned operating/control cost separate.
- Upstream CI and image build/push success reduce uncertainty about the reviewed source artifact's configured build path, while leaving organization intake, deployment and operating cost correctly unresolved.

### Decision Insights

1. **Decide edition/integration before approving unit economics.** The required API/embed path is simultaneously an unresolved contract and a public per-completion charge. A wrong edition assumption can invalidate both architecture and cost. Smallest next action: OI-001/OI-005 release-specific entitlement proof plus EXP-OI-003 quote/agreement.
2. **Define workload and assurance before comparing Community with Pro.** Completion volume, document size/retention, seat count, identity method and signature trust choice drive different cost lanes. Applying list prices without these quantities would produce false precision. Smallest next proof: EXP-OI-001 low/base/high workload tied to OI-002/OI-009/OI-011 decisions and Scalability/Continuity outputs.
3. **Treat control closure as part of cost, not a separate afterthought.** Self-hosting transfers runtime, security, recovery, privacy, maintenance and on-call work to the organization. Excluding those tasks makes the free-license path incomparable. Smallest next action: price the accepted OI-002–OI-013 closure plan with explicit internal/external ownership.
4. **Assign commercial continuity before any purchase.** Dynamic public terms do not establish license validation, grace, metering, transition or support. Without an owner and thresholds, a commercial event can interrupt revenue-critical onboarding. Smallest next action: EXP-OI-002 RACI/guardrails after EXP-OI-003 vendor evidence.

## Bounded Conclusion And Downstream Guidance

The approved evidence supports **continue evaluation conditionally**, not commercial or production approval. It identifies a zero-list-license Community path, a Pro seat plus usage-meter path for required API/embedding, optional identity/signature unit charges, approximate infrastructure dimensions, substantial organization-owned operating/control work, and confirmed continuity targets. It does **not** establish actual spend, a defensible total-cost range, negotiated commitment, renewal, owner, target design, achieved availability/RPO, or commercial-continuity behavior.

Business Continuity and Scalability should use the confirmed 99.5%/99% monthly availability, two-hour RPO, synchronous-preference, and pause-permitted targets to establish recovery/transfer and workload/capacity inputs. Revenue Risk should use the resulting expense model and vendor-interruption boundary, but must not infer revenue loss, cost magnitude, probability, vendor kill-switch behavior, or contractual remedies from targets, list prices, and source dependencies.
