# Contributor And Vendor Value: Feature-Level Contribution Evidence

Coordinator mapping: this bounded 16-unit author-name-grouped attribution sample is serialized as canonical E-059. Local FC labels remain for exact packet navigation.

Use when: A reviewer needs bounded, feature-level evidence of who materially delivered supported change units in the pinned public repository.
Reader question: Which Git author-name groups have direct, supportable contribution attribution across the project lifetime and consecutive cutoff-anchored periods?
Do not infer: Employment, staffing, authority, performance, effort, compensation, review quality, or vendor dependency from these units or aggregates.

## Scope And Evidence Boundary

- Product boundary: `docusealco/docuseal` release `3.1.7`, commit `a2d8b855491793870b7b4acf176d2d95ae95ff83`; cutoff 2026-08-06, America/Toronto.
- Sources: pinned local Git and source, the [shared GitHub/history/hosted-CI packet](github-history-and-hosted-ci.md), and Product Value's source-bounded outcome context. No CodeGraph or personnel sources were used.
- Selection: 16 representative feature/change units for which a coherent outcome, dated authorship, and exact source locator were defensible. This is deliberately not an exhaustive reconstruction of 2,879 reachable commits.
- Bands are within-audit weighting only: critical `8`, high `5`, meaningful `3`, bounded `2`, minor `1`. They weight supported product/change significance, not people or performance.
- A share allocates one unit's band points among directly evidenced Git author-name groups. Identical author names across different email labels are intentionally grouped for this bounded view; that grouping does not establish one person, account, role, or continuing identity. It is not time, effort, merit, ownership, or compensation. Single-author-name units receive 100%; multi-author-name shares reflect separable material sub-deliveries visible in the listed commits. Merge-only activity is not credited.
- Periods are consecutive and cutoff-anchored as directed: 2025-08-07–2026-08-06; 2024-08-07–2025-08-06; 2023-08-07–2024-08-06; and partial 2023-05-21–2023-08-06. Commit dates below are the repository's ISO author dates; each selected commit falls in its stated period when evaluated at the cutoff timezone.

## Material Unknowns And Access Limits

- These aggregates establish concentrated attribution within this judgment-selected, Git-author-name-grouped feature sample. They do not establish knowledge concentration, employment, vendor staffing, maintainer authority, bus factor, effort, individual performance, or the value of unselected work.
- Git author names/emails can be aliases, rebases, automation, shared identities, or changed addresses. Identical author names across multiple email labels were grouped deliberately; `DocuSeal` and `aqilaziz` remain separate author-name groups. No group is treated as proof of one person, account, role, continuing identity, or affiliation.
- Public history repeatedly merges work labeled `docusealco/wip`; that source repository, its contributors, review discussions, design work, testing, support, operations, and uncredited vendor labor were unavailable. Most selected units have no traceable public PR, issue, or submitted review.
- A commit author receives credit only for a dated, material sub-delivery listed above. Merger, commenter, release author, or later file editor status was not credited by default. Cross-period units were separated by dated sub-deliveries; no undated share was apportioned across periods.
- “Implemented” means inspectable in pinned source. It does not mean demonstrated on the auditor's web/mobile paths, accepted by customers/specialists, legally sufficient, secure in production, or proven operationally effective.
- The pinned release's hosted ESLint, Rubocop, Brakeman, Erblint, RSpec, and Docker jobs passed, but those repository-level results do not retroactively prove each unit's delivery quality. No coverage percentage, review-protection state, load test, recovery drill, production telemetry, or target acceptance evidence was available.
- The reviewer may use the sample attribution pattern and unmapped author-name/credit boundaries only as a trigger to verify succession and transfer. It must not rank people from these points or convert the bounded sample into a knowledge, staffing, person-dependency, performance, compensation, or vendor-value conclusion.

## Reuse Guidance

Reuse this bounded sample only for the stated succession and transfer questions, with its author-name grouping and selection limits intact. Do not convert the points into judgments about people, effort, staffing, ownership, performance, compensation, or vendor value.
