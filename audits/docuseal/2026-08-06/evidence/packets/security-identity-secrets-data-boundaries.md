## Observations

### SEC-ID-01 — Community human identity and session policy

- **Statement / exact locator:** `User` enables Devise password, recovery, remember, tracking, lock, and TOTP modules (`docuseal/app/models/user.rb:49-88`, `User`); the pinned policy sets bcrypt stretches to 10 outside tests, password length `6..128`, reset tokens to six hours, and remembered sessions to `SESSION_REMEMBER_DAYS` or 730 days (`docuseal/config/initializers/devise.rb:150-155,192-204,206-208,246-258`). The user object always requests remember-me (`docuseal/app/models/user.rb:91-93`). Valid and invalid password/TOTP cases are specified in `docuseal/spec/system/sign_in_spec.rb:11-57`.
- **Status / limitation:** **Observed declaration and test intent.** No test was run for this packet; live password hashing parameters, cookie settings, session age, lockout, and MFA adoption are unknown. The source does not establish that the defaults meet the organization's policy.
- **Affected boundary / history target:** Organization operator identity and session; `User` Devise declaration and `config/initializers/devise.rb` remember/password settings.
- **Likely control/artifact trigger:** Organization IAM standard and access-control matrix covering password strength, session/reauthentication, lockout, recovery, emergency access, and evidence retention.

### SEC-ID-02 — MFA is per-user; `FORCE_MFA` is a dashboard redirect

- **Statement / exact locator:** MFA enrollment validates a TOTP before enabling it, and removal requires a current TOTP (`docuseal/app/controllers/mfa_setup_controller.rb:16-43`); provisioning persists an `otp_secret` before activation (`:49-56`). `FORCE_MFA` is checked by `DashboardController#maybe_redirect_mfa_setup`, which redirects a signed-in user from the dashboard when no MFA is enabled (`docuseal/app/controllers/dashboard_controller.rb:3-10,28-37`). Other authenticated controllers inherit `ApplicationController` authentication/authorization, but the targeted source search found no global `FORCE_MFA` guard (`docuseal/app/controllers/application_controller.rb:9-15`).
- **Status / limitation:** **Observed; enforcement scope inferred.** The code proves the location of the Community enforcement hook, not every possible extension or Pro hook. It does not prove organization-wide MFA, recovery governance, or a live control.
- **Affected boundary / history target:** Privileged human authentication; `MfaSetupController` and `DashboardController#maybe_redirect_mfa_setup`.
- **Likely control/artifact trigger:** MFA enforcement design and negative-route tests demonstrating that an unenrolled user cannot reach any privileged action, not just the dashboard.

### SEC-ID-03 — Community authorization is account-scoped but has one declared role

- **Statement / exact locator:** Community declares only `admin`; new or invalid roles are normalized to `admin` (`docuseal/app/models/user.rb:49-52,74-79`; `docuseal/app/controllers/users_controller.rb:38-60,106-120`). `Ability` grants account-scoped management of users, account/configuration, templates, submissions, submitters, webhooks, and the user's own tokens/configuration (`docuseal/lib/ability.rb:3-27`). Controller loading and authorization is enforced centrally for non-Devise UI routes (`docuseal/app/controllers/application_controller.rb:9-15`) and on API controllers (`docuseal/app/controllers/api/api_base_controller.rb:13-16`).
- **Status / limitation:** **Observed.** Account predicates establish the intended tenant boundary, not live segregation or least privilege. No Community separation of template authors, support operators, security administrators, and data administrators is declared in these paths.
- **Affected boundary / history target:** Operator authorization and tenant isolation; `User::ROLES`, `UsersController#role_valid?`, and `Ability#initialize`.
- **Likely control/artifact trigger:** Role/segregation-of-duties matrix, privileged-action approval and audit requirements, periodic access review, and an edition decision.

### SEC-ID-04 — SSO, roles, and SMS verification are public Pro claims, not assessed Community behavior

- **Statement / exact locator:** Community's SAML settings controller renders an unlock placeholder and exposes no SAML implementation (`docuseal/app/controllers/sso_settings_controller.rb:3-14`; `docuseal/app/views/sso_settings/_placeholder.html.erb:1-14`). The approved pricing page lists on-premises Pro features including user roles/teams, SSO/SAML, SMS identity verification, API, and embedding ([Plans and Pricing, “On-prem” and “Access and permissions”](https://www.docuseal.com/pricing)). The security page separately describes SAML for Pro/Enterprise ([Security, “SAML SSO”](https://www.docuseal.com/security)).
- **Status / limitation:** **Observed Community placeholder; documented Pro packaging, post-cutoff-validation.** The public pages are dynamic and not release-bound. They do not establish Pro implementation quality, entitlement terms, provisioning/deprovisioning, SCIM, role semantics, or control effectiveness.
- **Affected boundary / history target:** Community-to-Pro enterprise identity boundary; `SsoSettingsController#index` and public edition matrix.
- **Likely control/artifact trigger:** Deferred vendor-validation request for release-specific SAML protocol/security configuration, role catalog, joiner/mover/leaver and SCIM behavior, audit logs, test evidence, and support commitments.

### SEC-ID-05 — User and account removal are initially soft-deactivation

- **Statement / exact locator:** User removal sets `archived_at`; an archived user can be restored on recreation, while `active_for_authentication?` rejects archived users or users of archived accounts (`docuseal/app/controllers/users_controller.rb:38-46,94-101`; `docuseal/app/models/user.rb:77-88`). Account deletion locks and rewrites the initiating user's email and sets the account's `archived_at`, then states that permanent deletion will occur within two months (`docuseal/app/controllers/accounts_controller.rb:46-60`). A targeted search of `docuseal/app`, `docuseal/lib`, `docuseal/config`, and `docuseal/spec` found no Community job or service implementing that later account purge.
- **Status / limitation:** **Observed soft-deactivation; bounded source absence for later purge.** This does not prove a purge never occurs in Pro, Cloud, unavailable operational tooling, or live infrastructure. It also does not establish immediate token/session revocation outcomes.
- **Affected boundary / history target:** Joiner/mover/leaver and account lifecycle; `UsersController#destroy`, `User#active_for_authentication?`, `AccountsController#destroy`.
- **Likely control/artifact trigger:** Deprovisioning/revocation runbook, session and token invalidation test, account-erasure workflow, owner, SLA, evidence of completion, and exception/legal-hold handling.

### SEC-ID-06 — API identity is a broad, per-user bearer credential

- **Statement / exact locator:** API authentication hashes `X-Auth-Token` with SHA-256 and joins it to an active user (`docuseal/app/controllers/api/api_base_controller.rb:84-99`). The token plaintext is Active Record-encrypted and its lookup digest is stored separately (`docuseal/app/models/access_token.rb:23-38`). A user can replace the token; revealing it requires the user's password and is rate-limited (`docuseal/app/controllers/api_settings_controller.rb:3-16`; `docuseal/app/controllers/reveal_access_token_controller.rb:3-28`). API authorization then uses the same account-wide `Ability` (`docuseal/lib/ability.rb:14-24`). Request specs specify a forbidden result across production/testing account boundaries (`docuseal/spec/requests/submissions_spec.rb:39-72`; `docuseal/spec/requests/submitters_spec.rb:38-76`).
- **Status / limitation:** **Observed declaration and test intent.** No token expiry, scopes, per-integration purpose, issuance approval, or overlap-based rotation contract appears in these paths. The API lookup applies `User.active` but does not invoke `User#active_for_authentication?` or explicitly filter `Account.active`; the behavior of a token belonging to a non-archived user under an archived account therefore requires a focused test and remediation decision, not an exploitability assumption.
- **Affected boundary / history target:** Machine identity/API credential; `Api::ApiBaseController#current_user`, `AccessToken#set_sha256`, and `ApiSettingsController#create`.
- **Likely control/artifact trigger:** Machine-identity standard with dedicated integration principals, least scopes, expiry, rotation overlap, revocation, distribution, monitoring, and archived-account negative tests.

### SEC-ID-07 — Signer access is capability-link based unless optional email checks are configured

- **Statement / exact locator:** Public signer routes bypass normal user authentication and load a submitter by a unique 14-character Base58 slug (`docuseal/app/controllers/submit_form_controller.rb:3-14,139-145`; `docuseal/app/models/submitter.rb:42-59`). `Submitters::AuthorizedForForm` allows access when email/link 2FA preferences are not enabled; when enabled it accepts an encrypted cookie or purpose-bound signed token, with a same-account user exception for shared-link 2FA (`docuseal/lib/submitters/authorized_for_form.rb:3-43`). The email verification controller records `email_verified` and sets an encrypted, HTTP-only production-secure cookie for 12 hours (`docuseal/app/controllers/submit_form_email_2fas_controller.rb:3-30`); the system spec exercises valid and invalid email OTP flows (`docuseal/spec/system/signing_form_spec.rb:1206-1274`).
- **Status / limitation:** **Observed declaration and test intent.** Possession of the slug is the default Community signer capability in these paths; optional email OTP demonstrates control of an email channel, not binding to a KYC identity. Link entropy, delivery exposure, replay, and live cookie policy were not dynamically tested.
- **Affected boundary / history target:** External signer identity and document-access capability; `Submitter#slug`, `Submitters::AuthorizedForForm`, and `SubmitFormEmail2fasController`.
- **Likely control/artifact trigger:** Signer-assurance profile mapping transaction risk to link-only, email OTP, SMS, KBA, ID verification, or other proof; secure link delivery/expiration/revocation controls; specialist determination of KYC sufficiency.

### SEC-ID-08 — First-run bootstrap claims the initial operator and provisions signing material

- **Statement / exact locator:** Setup bypasses user authentication while `User.exists?` is false; creation saves the first user, generates signing certificates, stores them in encrypted account configuration, and signs the user in (`docuseal/app/controllers/setup_controller.rb:3-15,17-45,69-75`). The source check and create sequence are not wrapped in a repository-declared single bootstrap transaction or deployment network gate.
- **Status / limitation:** **Observed implementation; concurrency/exposure risk inferred.** Source does not establish whether a production platform isolates first boot, serializes setup requests, or records an approved operator. No live bootstrap was observed.
- **Affected boundary / history target:** Initial privileged identity and signing-secret provisioning; `SetupController#create` and `ensure_first_user_not_created!`.
- **Likely control/artifact trigger:** First-start runbook, temporary network isolation, nominated operator, concurrency test, custody record, closure verification, and alerting on later setup access.

### SEC-ID-09 — One root secret feeds multiple security purposes

- **Statement / exact locator:** Production accepts environment secrets, fetches newline-delimited `key=value` material from AWS Secrets Manager when `AWS_SECRET_MANAGER_ID` is set, or creates `docuseal.env` with a random `SECRET_KEY_BASE`; owner/mode `0600` changes are attempted only when root (`docuseal/config/dotenv.rb:18-60`). When `ENCRYPTION_SECRET` is absent, Active Record encryption derives its keys and salt from `SECRET_KEY_BASE` (`docuseal/config/environments/production.rb:116-124`). The same Rails root is used by Devise, JWT signing, form-email OTP derivation, submission tracking, and the default local Redis password (`docuseal/config/initializers/devise.rb:43-48`; `docuseal/lib/json_web_token.rb:3-12`; `docuseal/lib/email_verification_codes.rb:3-25`; `docuseal/lib/submission_events.rb:3-11`; `docuseal/config/dotenv.rb:103-110`).
- **Status / limitation:** **Observed declaration.** No secret values were inspected. Source does not establish AWS workload identity/IAM scope, secret schema validation, reload, rotation, key versions, old-key support, error recovery, audit logging, or actual file permissions.
- **Affected boundary / history target:** Application secret root and consumers; `config/dotenv.rb`, production Active Record encryption stanza, and named consumers.
- **Likely control/artifact trigger:** Secret/key inventory, purpose separation, managed workload identity, least-privilege retrieval, versioned rotation with backward decryption/verification, restart semantics, break-glass recovery, and a decision prohibiting or accepting persistent dotenv fallback.

### SEC-ID-10 — Field-level encryption is selective; signing key and password share one encrypted record

- **Statement / exact locator:** Active Record encryption is explicitly declared for access-token plaintext, account/user encrypted configuration, and webhook URL/header/HMAC material (`docuseal/app/models/access_token.rb:23-38`; `docuseal/app/models/encrypted_config.rb:23-36`; `docuseal/app/models/encrypted_user_config.rb:23-28`; `docuseal/app/models/webhook_url.rb:41-60`). Signing PKCS#12 bytes and the corresponding password are stored together inside one encrypted `EncryptedConfig` value (`docuseal/app/controllers/esign_settings_controller.rb:96-115`) and loaded together for use (`docuseal/lib/accounts.rb:108-131`). A repository-wide targeted `encrypts` search found no model-level Active Record encryption declaration for `User` identity/TOTP fields, `Submitter` PII/form values, `SubmissionEvent#data`, `SearchEntry`, or `DocumentMetadata`.
- **Status / limitation:** **Observed encryption declarations; bounded absence of model-level declarations.** Database-, disk-, object-store-, volume-, backup-, or provider-level encryption may exist but was not supplied. Application encryption does not establish custody, separation, rotation, or control effectiveness.
- **Affected boundary / history target:** Database-resident secrets, signing material, and personal data; model `encrypts` declarations and `EsignSettingsController#save_new_cert!`.
- **Likely control/artifact trigger:** Data/secret cryptographic inventory, KMS/HSM decision, key/password separation, non-exportable signing-key requirement, envelope/volume encryption, backup-key coupling, rotation and revocation, and restore validation.

### SEC-ID-11 — Sensitive document and PII data crosses SQL, blob storage, derivative indexes, and audit events

- **Statement / exact locator:** `Submitter` persists name, email, phone, IP, user agent, metadata, preferences, and arbitrary form values, and attaches documents/uploads/previews (`docuseal/app/models/submitter.rb:3-27,42-65`). `Submission` attaches the audit trail and result/source documents (`docuseal/app/models/submission.rb:44-75`). Tracking events persist request IP, user agent, session identifier, signed-in user ID, and event-specific data (`docuseal/lib/submission_events.rb:14-21`; `docuseal/app/models/submission_event.rb:31-69`). Full-text search derives searchable vectors from submitter email, phone, name, and short string field values (`docuseal/lib/search_entries.rb:132-183`; `docuseal/app/models/search_entry.rb:3-28`), while document metadata can persist extracted PDF text objects and coordinates keyed by account/checksum (`docuseal/lib/document_metadatas.rb:6-33`; `docuseal/app/models/document_metadata.rb:3-26`).
- **Status / limitation:** **Observed data classes and copies.** This is not an exhaustive business data inventory, and source does not establish whether target forms contain KYC documents or special-category data. Purpose, minimization, encryption, retention, backup deletion, and residency for each copy are unknown.
- **Affected boundary / history target:** Signer ingress → SQL fields/events/search derivatives + Active Storage blobs/document-text derivatives; `Submitter`, `Submission`, `SubmissionEvents.create_with_tracking_data`, `SearchEntries.index_submitter`, and `DocumentMetadatas.build_text_runs`.
- **Likely control/artifact trigger:** Data-flow diagram and record of processing/data inventory naming each primary and derivative store, purpose, classification, residency, encryption, access, retention, legal hold, backup, export, correction, and deletion verification.

### SEC-ID-12 — Storage privacy is configurable; live at-rest and lifecycle controls are unknown

- **Statement / exact locator:** Production selects S3, GCS, Azure, or disk based on environment configuration and defaults to disk; file delivery is proxied unless `ACTIVE_STORAGE_PUBLIC=true`, and service URLs default to 240 minutes (`docuseal/config/environments/production.rb:39-52`; `docuseal/config/storage.yml:1-37`). Provider credentials can be supplied by environment or loaded from an encrypted database configuration and installed into Active Storage at runtime (`docuseal/config/storage.yml:6-33`; `docuseal/lib/load_active_storage_configs.rb:18-46`; management path `docuseal/app/controllers/storage_settings_controller.rb:3-33`). Public signer download helpers create shorter five-minute proxy paths (`docuseal/lib/submitters.rb:28,265-293`).
- **Status / limitation:** **Observed configuration options.** No approved evidence identifies the target store, public/private setting, bucket/volume policy, at-rest key, credential identity, versioning, replication, object lock, lifecycle, deletion, backup, or restore state.
- **Affected boundary / history target:** SQL ↔ Active Storage provider ↔ signed/proxy download consumers; production storage stanza, `storage.yml`, and `LoadActiveStorageConfigs.reload`.
- **Likely control/artifact trigger:** Storage/security ADR and deploy-time assertions for private access, URL lifetime, managed identity, encryption key ownership, residency, versioning, retention, legal hold, backup/restore, and provider migration.

### SEC-ID-13 — Webhooks can export PII, field values, documents, and audit links; entitlement remains Pro

- **Statement / exact locator:** The implemented webhook serializer includes submitter email, phone, name, IP, user agent, metadata, field values, document URLs, audit-log URLs, variables, and preview URLs (`docuseal/lib/submitters/serialize_for_webhook.rb:3-48,51-125`). Delivery posts that JSON to the configured URL, adds configured secret headers, and applies an HMAC signature (`docuseal/lib/send_webhook_request.rb:17-50`); form completion invokes that serializer (`docuseal/app/jobs/send_form_completed_webhook_request_job.rb:10-39`). The approved pricing page states API and embedding are Pro on-premises capabilities and lists webhook functions with Pro ([Plans and Pricing, “On-prem” and FAQ](https://www.docuseal.com/pricing)).
- **Status / limitation:** **Observed Community source path; documented Pro packaging, post-cutoff-validation.** Source presence does not prove Community entitlement or live enablement, and unavailable Pro behavior is not assessed. Destination approval, data minimization, receiver security, residency, delivery history, and retention are unknown.
- **Affected boundary / history target:** Internal signer/submission data → configured external webhook consumer; `Submitters::SerializeForWebhook.call`, `SendWebhookRequest.call`, public edition matrix.
- **Likely control/artifact trigger:** Deferred vendor entitlement/implementation evidence plus an organization egress inventory, destination allow-list, payload minimization, DPA/residency review, receiver authentication, secret rotation, replay/idempotency, monitoring, and retention controls.

### SEC-ID-14 — Archive is not deletion, and self-hosted privacy obligations remain with the customer

- **Statement / exact locator:** Submission and template deletion archive by default and destroy only when `permanently=true` (`docuseal/app/controllers/submissions_controller.rb:72-87`; `docuseal/app/controllers/api/submissions_controller.rb:126-136`; `docuseal/app/controllers/templates_controller.rb:70-85`). The model graph declares dependent destruction for submissions, submitters, events, templates, searches, and several configuration records (`docuseal/app/models/submission.rb:44-75`; `docuseal/app/models/submitter.rb:42-71`; `docuseal/app/models/account.rb:20-54`), but source alone does not prove blob/backend/backup erasure or verification. The public privacy/GDPR pages describe service/Cloud retention and data-subject rights ([Privacy Policy, “Data Retention” and “Your Rights”](https://www.docuseal.com/privacy); [GDPR Compliance, “Our Compliance as Data Processors”](https://www.docuseal.com/privacy/gdpr)). The Terms explicitly state that storage, access, sharing, and deletion terms do not apply to self-hosted deployments because the customer solely manages data in its environment ([Terms and Conditions, introductory self-hosted paragraph](https://www.docuseal.com/terms)).
- **Status / limitation:** **Observed archive/destroy paths; documented public responsibility boundary, post-cutoff-validation.** Public policies are dynamic and primarily Cloud/service-oriented; they do not establish Community procedures or resolve the organization's controller/processor, residency, retention, legal-hold, or erasure duties. Rails association declarations do not prove physical or backup deletion.
- **Affected boundary / history target:** Active records → archive → optional application destroy → SQL/blob/derivative/backup lifecycle; delete controllers and model dependencies.
- **Likely control/artifact trigger:** Organization-owned privacy lifecycle procedure and specialist-approved retention schedule mapping access, export, correction, restriction, legal hold, archive, permanent deletion, blob/derivative cleanup, backup expiry, and evidence of verified erasure.
