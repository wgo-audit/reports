### Source-Bounded Findings

1. The core signer workflow is materially implemented: public slug access, optional email OTP, lifecycle guards, required-field checks, signature-image modalities, ordered multi-signer completion, result generation, audit generation, email, API serialization, downloads, and webhooks. Signature capture and `complete_form` do not prove disclosure, consent, intent, or legal effect. The source does not prove that this workflow has been demonstrated on the organization's web/mobile paths.
2. Community operator authorization is not granular: every enabled Community user role is `admin`, and `Ability` grants broad account management. Signer email OTP is implemented, but phone identity, SSO/SAML, and additional operator roles are not assessable Pro capabilities.
3. Integration evidence conflicts at the applicability layer. Community implements API/webhook surfaces, while README/public pricing place API, embedding, SSO/roles/SMS in Pro, and the checked-in OpenAPI mixes hosted and Pro-only paths. A release-specific entitlement and compatibility matrix is required before architecture selection.
4. The output model supports useful evidence but is conditional and snapshot-based. A generated result hash and optional embedded PDF signature are distinct from the audit PDF, relational event history for which no append-only control was found, and the verification endpoint's unscoped checksum membership result.
5. Public statements materially exceed what the inspected Community source and evidence can establish. Claims of legal binding, independent verification, compliance, unchanged state, and identity proof remain public promises pending edition-specific artifact tests and legal/compliance/security determinations.
6. File/image intake can carry customer-supplied attachments, but the Community source does not make the product decision that such files are verified KYC evidence or prove malware/content validation, identity binding, authenticity, or retention acceptance.

## Material Unknowns And Access Limits

- Demonstration/mobile/customer acceptance: create controlled web and mobile fixtures, exercise success and failure paths, and retain outputs/event records.
- Edition/entitlement: obtain a release-specific Community/Pro contract and component inventory from DocuSeal.
- Evidence acceptance: run signed and unsigned artifact tests with approved certificates/TSA/trust roots; test retention, restore, purge, and tenant-bound verification; route results to legal/compliance/CISO.
- Public claims: obtain the exact assurance/legal basis and edition scope; the public pages themselves are not adopted as audit conclusions.
