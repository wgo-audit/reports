## Material Unknowns And Access Limits

- **Actual cash exposure is unknown.** No approved evidence supplies current spend, committed spend, budget, target volumes, Pro seats, selected paid identity/signature options, discounts, taxes, currency, infrastructure rate card, staffing allocation, support plan, renewal date, or billing owner.
- **The public `$0` Community license price does not establish a `$0` total-cost estimate or lower bound.** Self-hosted infrastructure, data protection, delivery, security, recovery, monitoring, maintenance, and staffing remain organization responsibilities. No numeric lower bound is supported.
- **The Pro list-price formula is identifiable but not populated:** monthly list exposure is `20 × Pro seats + 0.20 × production API/embed completions + selected metered identity/signature/AI charges`. This is a planning formula, not an estimate, because every organization quantity and negotiated term is unknown.
- **Commercial continuity is unknown.** No executed agreement establishes renewal, term, notice, license validation, non-payment grace, usage-meter dispute, price protection, data/export rights, transition assistance, support targets, or what continues operating after cancellation/termination.
- **Operating-cost range is unknown.** The approved vendor sizing examples are estimates only; they do not price the confirmed availability/RPO targets and omit the target architecture, retention, backup, security, recovery, compliance-control, network, and staffing requirements.
- **Continuity targets are now known, but their cost is not.** The organization requires 99.5% monthly signing availability, 99% monthly onboarding availability, a two-hour RPO with synchronous transactions preferred, and permits pausing all new onboarding during interruption. No design, measured control, resource quantity, or rate proves or prices those targets.

## Reuse Guidance

- Expense Exposure may use the public list prices only as `post-cutoff-validation` and must label formulas as unpopulated planning formulas, never actual or forecast spend.
- Scalability may reuse the approximate resource dimensions, not convert them to a benchmark or cost. Business Continuity may reuse the confirmed availability/RPO/continuation targets and the unknown license/billing interruption boundary, but must not infer achieved performance or a kill switch. Revenue Risk may reuse the metered completion dependency only after Product Value and Business Continuity evidence is reconciled.
- Architecture and Security dependencies are cost drivers, not evidence of spend. The Community/Pro decision, target architecture, approved control baseline, workload, vendor quote, and staffing model must be established before a decision-grade total-cost range is produced.
