### Decision Insights

1. **Do not accept the expanded August security-page claims as cutoff assurance.** The cutoff-bounded March version is materially less specific; effective-time conflict means daily backup, quarterly restore, formal BCP/DR, 24/7 on-call, incident-drill, and per-change rollback statements require independent cutoff evidence through [OI-021](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-021) and [OI-023](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-023).
2. **Make recovery proof cross-store and outcome-based.** PostgreSQL, ClickHouse, object storage, buffered ingestion, asynchronous deletion, and queued work have different failure/consistency boundaries; a database snapshot claim alone cannot close [OI-021](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-021).
3. **Establish successor control before treating automation as resilience.** Workflows and integrations can deepen dependence on account, credential, MFA, billing, and publication authority. [OI-022](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-022) requires a primary/backup ownership register, safe access checks, and a provider/person-loss tabletop.
4. **Test the entire detection-to-closure chain, not individual tools.** Source offers useful probes and routes, but only enabled state, delivery, acknowledgement, response, recovery validation, customer reconciliation, and follow-up can close [OI-023](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-023).
