### Decision Insights

- **Positive CTO signal:** Public product/source alignment, candid limitations, and user-facing issue history support a positive view of product rigor. Use the first month to learn customer-effective capabilities, privacy semantics, raw-export fulfillment, and event-durability trade-offs through [OI-001](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-001), [OI-009](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-009), [OI-010](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-010), and [OI-011](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-011); private evidence is neutral at offer stage.
- **First 30 days:** Freeze broad repackaging until product, billing, support, and engineering produce one versioned plan/edition/variant matrix and sample current/grandfathered tenant results. In parallel, resolve the salt-policy wording with privacy/legal and demonstrate one scheduled raw-export delivery plus one tracker-to-dashboard journey.
- **Immediate correction:** Confirm the deployed Events API success body, add a versioned contract test, and align docs or handler under [OI-012](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-012) without conflating response format with persistence durability.
- **Investment direction:** Preserve the candid constraints and shared query/tracker semantics; prioritize verification and contract ownership over a speculative rewrite. The visible product surface is broad but structurally coherent.

## Bounded Conclusion And Downstream Guidance

`[Verified audit conclusion]` Plausible's public product story is substantially represented in source: the tracker, query model, conversion tools, collaboration/SSO, plan gates, import/export modes, and Cloud/CE boundary are coherent and often candid about constraints. This supports a positive judgment about product rigor and user concern. Live fulfillment, customer-specific entitlements, privacy/legal assurance, and adoption are private onboarding matters; the practical work is to preserve the coherence, reconcile the few specific promise boundaries, and make event acceptance/durability explicit.

Revenue Risk should use the promise/entitlement gaps without inferring actual revenue, contract breach, or customer impact. Security/Privacy should use the data-input/salt conflict without inferring noncompliance. Business Continuity and Quality may use the workflow boundaries but must not assume production operation, SLO attainment, deployment, or acceptance.
