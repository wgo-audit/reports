# Maintenance Cost

## Audit Question, Depth, And Evidence Boundary

At detailed depth, this review asks what evidence-bounded skill mix, complexity/hotspots, operating burden, and safe-change path a small replacement team would inherit. It uses public `primary-code` at pinned commit `9cc669b97ece3ecd37fcb3950791cb3873d7944d`, cutoff 2026-08-22 22:08:28 EDT, its Git history and relevant public GitHub issue/PR/Actions records, the [delivery-and-quality packet](../../evidence/packets/delivery-and-quality.md), and direct E-078–E-081 evidence. Architecture, Code Quality, and Business Continuity handoffs provided navigation; their direct evidence and linked controls were checked before reuse.

This review does not convert file breadth, line size, churn, dependencies, Git identities, or public issue reports into defects, elapsed effort, headcount, staffing cost, individual performance, current ownership, or operating competence. No production, cloud, staff, access, contract, billing, runbook, incident, or recovery evidence was approved.

### Decision Insights

- **Onboarding decision:** how to learn production-change operation without disrupting a maintainable source base. **Causal relationship:** source-level maintainability is strongly evidenced, while promotion, rollback, recovery, and successor control are private. **Smallest next step:** after joining, walk one representative commit-to-runtime, migration/rollback, alert/reconciliation, and backup-operator path through [OI-003](../../controls/open-items.md) and [OI-021–OI-023](../../controls/open-items.md). Their public absence carries no negative weight.
- **Decision changed:** whether to prescribe a replacement-team size from public evidence. **Causal relationship:** the codebase spans several capability boundaries, but repository breadth contains no workload, role coverage, skill proficiency, time, rate, or cost observation. **Consequence of a wrong choice:** the mandate may be under-resourced or unjustifiably inflated. **Smallest next proof:** map critical services to current primary/backup coverage and observed workload under [OI-022](../../controls/open-items.md), then reconcile actual staffing/vendor cost under [OI-025](../../controls/open-items.md); do not derive a number from LOC, churn, or contributor history.

## Bounded Conclusion And Downstream Guidance

The public corpus establishes a broad but navigable source/technology surface, meaningful automated gates, explicit migration/release utilities, and concrete GitHub repair history. It also establishes that safe replacement maintenance crosses multiple data, client, edition, release, and operating boundaries. It does **not** establish required staffing, hours, cost, current capability coverage, live safety, or successor readiness. Project Health may use the source-bounded burden and proof gates; it must not treat file/churn counts as defect or productivity measures, infer team performance, or treat build/CI configuration as deployment and operating effectiveness. Code Quality retains defect/test truth, Business Continuity and Contributor/Vendor Value retain transfer/knowledge truth, and Expense Exposure retains cash impact.
