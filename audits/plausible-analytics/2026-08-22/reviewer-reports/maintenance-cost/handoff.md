# Maintenance Cost Handoff

## Confirmed Navigation

Use the [report](report.md), [complexity/skills/time-to-safety map](../../controls/maintenance/complexity-skills-and-time-to-safety.md), [delivery/quality packet](../../evidence/packets/delivery-and-quality.md), and direct [E-078–E-081](../../evidence/evidence-ledger.md) evidence.

## Constraints And Conflicts

Source breadth, size, churn, and Git history are navigation evidence, not defects, headcount, hours, cost, ownership, or performance. CI and image builds are independent evidence; neither proves Cloud promotion or operation.

## Material Unknowns

Close linked [OI-001–OI-004, OI-008, OI-017, OI-021–OI-023, and OI-025](../../controls/open-items.md) for data safety, release, tests, credentials, recovery, response, successor, and cost proof. CE packaging/upgrade evidence is outside scope.

## Downstream Use

Project Health may use the capability boundaries, hotspot reading rule, and proof-gate sequence. Do not infer current staffing, competence, live readiness, or a numeric maintenance cost.
