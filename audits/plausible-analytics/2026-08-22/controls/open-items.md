# Open Items

## Reading Rule For A CTO Candidate

This register is an onboarding discovery and improvement index, not a defect count, organizational scorecard, or list of offer conditions. A `verification` item created solely because production, financial, contractual, or staff evidence is private is **neutral**: it does not imply the control is absent, the team is sloppy, or the candidate should delay acceptance. Its priority orders sensible discovery after joining if the topic matters to the role.

Give decision weight first to affirmative evidence of rigor and to direct, reproducible findings. `action` items record concrete source corrections; `decision-needed` items record explicit trade-offs; `verification` items usually record what an external audit cannot know. Only contrary internal evidence, concealment of a known problem, or persistent refusal to own a concrete issue should turn a verification question into a negative organizational signal.
