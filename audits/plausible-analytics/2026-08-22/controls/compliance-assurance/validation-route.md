# Compliance Assurance Validation Route

## Purpose And Stop Condition

This route exists because named GDPR/CCPA/PECR, contractual, privacy, security, supply-chain and licensing claims require legal interpretation, customer-effective scope, live proof or external assurance unavailable in the approved public corpus. It is not legal advice, a control test, or a certification plan.

The incoming CTO should not accept sole authority to repeat categorical privacy/compliance claims until [OI-028](../open-items.md#oi-028) has a named executive sponsor, qualified privacy/licensing counsel, evidence owners, read-only access, and explicit authority to narrow or correct claims. Technical owner items remain independently open; a legal memo cannot substitute for control evidence, and a source fix cannot determine legal sufficiency.

## Evidence Handling And Decision Rules

1. Freeze the exact public/customer wording and effective date before collecting evidence. The currently served August 2026 privacy/security bodies and undated compliance page cannot backfill the cutoff versions. The separately dated `2026-08-02` legal assessment is cutoff-eligible attributed analysis, not certification or retrofit control proof.
2. Classify each row as public commitment, implementation, live state, customer-effective contract, specialist judgment, or external attestation; never let one stand in for another.
3. Give technical validation to the reviewer/owner already responsible. Compliance Assurance consumes their bounded conclusion and maps it; it does not rerun an exploit, restore, IAM review, scan, deployment, or customer-data exercise.
4. Record both supportive and contradictory evidence. Claim correction is a valid closure; lack of certification is not by itself a defect.
5. Keep current customer data and secrets out of the audit artifact. Retain redacted locators, reviewer identity/role, date, scope, exception and approval.
6. A fulfillment route closes only when applicable scope, direct evidence, required specialist judgment and accountable approval are recorded. A specific claim route may instead close through an approved withdrawal/correction or documented scope exclusion; preserve that disposition and effective version. Otherwise retain `partial`, `unknown`, or `awaiting-specialist-validation`.
