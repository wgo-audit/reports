# Requirement-to-Evidence Matrix

## Scope, Baselines, And Status Meaning

This matrix maps only requirements and claims named in the approved public Plausible corpus, at the audit cutoff `2026-08-22 22:08:28 EDT`. It does not import statutory text, decide legal applicability, certify compliance, or retest controls.

The cutoff-valid named baseline is Plausible's public DPA: effective `2020-10-21`, updated `2026-03`, and defining “Data Protection Legislation” as GDPR Regulation (EU) 2016/679 plus applicable visitor-data/privacy laws. The `2026-03` data policy additionally names GDPR, CCPA and PECR; the `2026-03` Terms incorporate the DPA. The cutoff-valid `2026-03` security page supplies provider-authored control claims. Source is pinned to `9cc669b97ece3ecd37fcb3950791cb3873d7944d`. [E-068](../../evidence/evidence-ledger.md#e-068), [E-046](../../evidence/evidence-ledger.md#e-046)

Status is deliberately narrow:

- `evidenced`: the cited public commitment or bounded implementation fact is directly supported; this never means compliance.
- `partial`: some named commitment has mapped implementation or public evidence, but effective scope or fulfillment is unproved.
- `unknown`: the approved corpus cannot establish the result.
- `awaiting-specialist-validation`: a legal, privacy, licensing, audit, or other qualified judgment is required.
- `not-in-scope`: the item is outside this cutoff or approved source boundary.
