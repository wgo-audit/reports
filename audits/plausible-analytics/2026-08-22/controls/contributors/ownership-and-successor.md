# Contribution Concentration And Successor Evidence View

## Evidence Boundary

Reader question: what does public evidence establish about contribution concentration, accountable ownership, and successor readiness for value-critical Plausible surfaces?

This view uses the feature-level sample in [contribution-value.md](contribution-value.md), pinned repository governance/security files, and the Business Continuity dependency boundary. It contains implementation/history and public role/channel evidence. It contains no approved staff roster, employment/vendor record, role charter, repository permission, cloud/account control, knowledge map, contract, acceptance record, or tested handoff. It must not be used to infer performance, availability, identity, ownership, or indispensable-person status ([E-073 and E-077](../../evidence/evidence-ledger.md)). Evidence/open-item links open the canonical table file because table rows do not provide Markdown anchors; the label identifies the exact row.

## Evidence Dimensions Used

## Material Unknowns And Closure Routes

- [OI-022](../open-items.md) already owns the distinct missing proof: a minimum critical-service register, current primary/backup operators, least-privilege access, credential/MFA custody, break-glass and provider-exit paths, plus representative successor testing. No duplicate open item was created.
- A `vendor-ownership-commercial` packet was not requested because no approved contract, billing, usage, staffing, role, acceptance, renewal, or account-control record existed to populate it. Public dependency presence is not vendor value or spend evidence.
- The public contribution sample supports a **verification priority**, not an employment, retention, promotion, termination, compensation, outsourcing, or vendor-renewal decision.
