# Burn, Renewal, And Vendor-Control Boundary

## Purpose And Evidence Boundary

This source-bounded view answers which cash and interruption exposures are actually evidenced, which are only potential, and what belongs in the incoming CTO's normal first-month budget and vendor baseline. The cutoff is 2026-08-22 22:08:28 EDT. Public source, public customer terms, and pre-cutoff provider incidents are present; bills, contracts, payroll, live usage, production topology, customer mix, margins, account ownership, and approval records are not. That private-data boundary is neutral and supports no financial-health or offer-stage inference.

The conditional view is triggered by provider-interruption evidence with potential business consequence in [E-050](../../evidence/evidence-ledger.md#e-050) and by source-visible payment and customer-delivery dependencies. The incidents' cash effect and financial materiality are unknown. This view does **not** quantify burn. No `vendor-ownership-commercial` packet was requested because the audit approved no billing, usage, contract, renewal, or vendor-control record that such a collector could inspect.

## First Decision Packet

Before approving a budget, pricing/capacity change, or inherited vendor mandate, obtain the redacted packet in [OI-025](../open-items.md#oi-025): trailing-12-month vendor and staffing spend; monthly usage and unit rates; credits/discounts/taxes; commitments, overages and renewal/termination dates; primary/backup commercial and technical owners; quotas and interruption paths; and cost allocation by traffic, storage, retention, export and service tier. Reconcile it with [OI-009](../open-items.md#oi-009) for customer-effective entitlements and [OI-022](../open-items.md#oi-022) for successor access.

## Decision Boundary

Public evidence supports a **potential-exposure map**, not a burn estimate or financial-health conclusion. After joining, the CTO should build an expense/usage/contract baseline and clarify authority over budgets, renewal owners, quotas, capacity guardrails, and pricing/claim corrections. The lack of such private material in a public audit does not count against Plausible. No renegotiation, purchase, cancellation, quota change, or vendor action is authorized by this audit.
