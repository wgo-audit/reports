# Maintenance Cost

## Audit Question, Depth, And Evidence Boundary

This detailed review asks what skill mix, recurring effort, operating burden, safe-change risk, and successor time-to-safety a small library team should expect across Run, Subscribe, and Replace. The cutoff is 2026-08-20 at onboarding start, America/Toronto. Evidence is the approved `primary-code` snapshot at commit `9cc669b97ece3ecd37fcb3950791cb3873d7944d`, direct maintenance-source evidence [E-051](../../evidence/evidence-ledger.md#e-051), and completed Architecture, Code Quality, Business Continuity, Scalability, Cloud Security, Application Security, and Expense evidence where directly relevant.

No library staffing, skill inventory, activity log, ticket history, on-call record, deployment, maintenance calendar, staff rate, support account, contract, replacement candidate, or live operation was inspected. No change, restore, upgrade, load test, or handoff was performed. This review does not make a staffing-cost fact, treat code size/complexity as a defect, or assume each capability requires a separate person.

## Coverage And Material Gaps

Coverage includes the application/tracker/toolchain skill surface, dual-store and cross-store migration care, source and artifact provenance, quality/reproduction burden, backup/recovery, queues/reports/deletion, capacity/telemetry, privacy/access/security upkeep, vendor/account/renewal/exit duties, proposed operating cadences, provisional time bands, and successor gates. The detailed [time-to-safety and care envelope](../../controls/maintenance/time-to-safety-and-care-envelope.md) separates source-backed duties from planning assumptions.

Actual effort and successor readiness remain unknown. [OI-020](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-020) routes an 8–12-week once-only activity log and time-boxed non-production handoff. [OI-015](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-015) retains ownership/accountability; [OI-017](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-017) retains actual hours/rates and comparable cash evidence. No rate or planning band is treated as cash.

## Mandate-Relevant Strengths

- The approved source pins core Erlang/Elixir/Node tool versions, documents a complete local setup path, and declares broad backend, CE, dashboard, tracker, migration, static, and artifact workflows. This gives a successor a real starting point, although the library-specific environment and deployment remain unproved ([E-009](../../evidence/evidence-ledger.md#e-009), [E-010](../../evidence/evidence-ledger.md#e-010), [E-051](../../evidence/evidence-ledger.md#e-051)).
- The source separates PostgreSQL and ClickHouse roles, named job queues, buffer/query/storage signals, release commands, and tracker code maps. Those explicit boundaries make the maintenance surface inventoryable rather than opaque ([E-003](../../evidence/evidence-ledger.md#e-003), [E-035](../../evidence/evidence-ledger.md#e-035), [E-047](../../evidence/evidence-ledger.md#e-047)–[E-051](../../evidence/evidence-ledger.md#e-051)).
- Subscribe can transfer infrastructure, datastore, backup, and platform patching if the accepted service/contract proves that transfer; the retained library duties are narrower and can be measured separately instead of assumed away ([E-039](../../evidence/evidence-ledger.md#e-039), [OI-015](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-015), [OI-020](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-020)).

### Decision Insights

1. **Test the successor route before treating Run as the cheaper option.** CE's absence of a Plausible fee coexists with two stores, explicit migrations, conditional release evidence, community-only support, and unmeasured care. A wrong assumption can turn savings into recovery or opportunity-cost exposure. Assign owners through [OI-015](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-015), then time the bounded Run handoff in [OI-020](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-020) before comparing cash through [OI-017](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-017).
2. **Plan Run upgrades as recovery changes, not routine image refreshes.** Latest-major.minor security support creates pressure to stay current, while cross-store migration and rollback limits require provenance, backup, restore, and stop conditions. Use [OI-004](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-004)/[OI-005](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-005) as upgrade acceptance gates; do not use `latest` alone as a control.
3. **Compare Subscribe on transferred and retained duties together.** Hosted operation can materially lower technical care, but the library still owns event governance, role/report correctness, quota/billing, assurance, escalation, and exit. Obtain the accepted topology/contract and measure the retained monthly work through [OI-017](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-017)/[OI-020](../../controls/open-items.md#oi-020) rather than subtracting the Run band mechanically.
4. **Keep Replace as a future requirements envelope, not a maintenance saving.** With no approved candidate, recurring care and transition effort are unknown. Preserve the same time-to-safety gates for a funded shortlist; do not credit a replacement for duties it has not evidenced.

## Bounded Conclusion And Downstream Guidance

Run has a plausible documented technical starting point and strong upstream CI, but its library-specific maintenance burden and successor route are unproved. A provisional reserve of 16–40 staff-hours/month plus 5–15 staff-days per significant CE upgrade/recovery rehearsal is only a transparent planning hypothesis until OI-020 measures it; incidents and change work remain extra. Under an accepted hosted topology and contract, Subscribe is hypothesized to narrow steady-state library care to 5–16 staff-hours/month after transition while retaining governance, reporting, account, assurance, quota, renewal, support, and exit duties. Replace has no evidence-based maintenance estimate.

Project Health and synthesis may use the duty map, explicit formulas, and time-to-safety gates. They must not treat the planning bands as measured effort, staffing sufficiency, cash, SLA, vendor performance, or a replacement advantage.
