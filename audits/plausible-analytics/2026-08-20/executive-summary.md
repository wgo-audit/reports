# Executive Summary

## Mandate, Boundary, And Bottom Line

Plausible is a credible privacy-first foundation for measuring library services. The reviewed source supports custom events, goals, trends, CSV/API outputs, a fixed monthly email summary, and role-separated dashboard access. It minimizes several conventional tracking practices by avoiding raw IP address and full User-Agent storage in the analytics tables. Those strengths do not prove the library's deployed configuration, durable event capture, effective governance, recovery, capacity, actual cost, or the hosted service's controls.

**Provisional recommendation:** make **Subscribe** the preferred direction to validate, not an approved migration. It offers the clearest potential reduction in the library's two-datastore, upgrade, recovery, security, and successor burden. Keep **Run** as the controlled interim and fallback only while the library establishes its exact deployment, loss tolerance, recovery/provenance, and care capacity. Defer **Replace** to a future funded shortlist, carrying forward the requirements in this audit; no approved evidence presently supports a product, cost, security, or continuity comparison for an alternative.

No option should receive unconditional approval today. Subscribe still requires security/privacy and procurement review, an accepted 18-site/25-member topology and Enterprise or multi-team quote, functional validation, responsibility transfer, and exit evidence. Run remains viable only if bounded non-production tests show that the existing deployment can meet an approved loss/outage threshold and be recovered and handed over safely.

## Material Risks, Unknowns, And Decisions

### Decision-Useful Conclusions

1. **Product fit turns on the meaning of “journey.”** CE can report separately configured stages and conversion rates; ordered funnels/path exploration are edition-bounded. This single decision can preserve Run as functionally plausible or establish a material gap. [Product Value, High/S](reviewer-reports/product-value/report.md), [OI-007](controls/open-items.md#oi-007).
2. **Measurement completeness requires a tolerance and one targeted failure proof.** HTTP 202 precedes the reviewed ClickHouse write, so receipt is not durability. The decision is not whether buffering exists, but whether the deployed loss and outage behavior fits an approved threshold. [Architecture, High/M](reviewer-reports/architecture/report.md), [Code Quality, High/M](reviewer-reports/code-quality/report.md), [OI-002](controls/open-items.md#oi-002)–[OI-003](controls/open-items.md#oi-003).
3. **Privacy-first design does not replace governance.** Configurable URLs/properties and alternate outputs can introduce sensitive content or excessive access; Sentry paths can also expand transient visitor or OAuth data. Approve the smallest data contract before adding journey events. [Security and Privacy, High/M](reviewer-reports/security-privacy/report.md), [Application Security, High/S](reviewer-reports/application-security/report.md), [OI-008](controls/open-items.md#oi-008)–[OI-013](controls/open-items.md#oi-013).
4. **Run's decisive risk is operational proof, not nominal traffic.** Two stores, cross-store migrations, buffered state, reports/deletion jobs, artifact provenance, recovery, and process-local sessions must be proven together. Annual averages and a result-free 6,000-rps fixture are not capacity evidence. [Business Continuity, High/M](reviewer-reports/business-continuity/report.md), [Scalability, High/M](reviewer-reports/scalability/report.md).
5. **Subscribe's value is verified duty transfer, not a public price or promise.** The assumed one-team topology exceeds published Business site/member limits, billable usage includes custom events, and standard terms do not prove an SLA or controls. A dated quote, assurance package, owners, escalation, and exit exercise are prerequisite. [Expense Exposure, High/S](reviewer-reports/expense-exposure/report.md), [Contributor and Vendor Value, High/M](reviewer-reports/contributor-vendor-value/report.md).
6. **Care and affordability are not yet measured.** The planning-hour bands are transparent hypotheses. An 8–12-week once-only activity log, timed successor handoff, actual Run records, hosted quote, and affordability ceiling are the smallest evidence set for a responsible financial decision. [Maintenance Cost](reviewer-reports/maintenance-cost/report.md), [OI-017](controls/open-items.md#oi-017)–[OI-020](controls/open-items.md#oi-020).
