## Bounded Fit Position

The source supports the library's baseline reporting, custom-event, goal, trend, and role needs in principle. Run is only a partial fit until the library decides whether ordered journey/drop-off analysis is mandatory. Monthly email is a fixed summary, not evidence of a customizable formal report. Subscribe may close the journey feature gap, but hosted plan entitlement and service acceptance remain unverified. Replace has no approved product evidence and cannot be compared functionally beyond naming unmet requirements.
