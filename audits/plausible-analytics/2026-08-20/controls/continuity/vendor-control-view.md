# Vendor Control And Exit View

Reader question: How do vendor/community dependencies change continuity and exit control across Run, Subscribe, and Replace?

## Evidence Boundary

This view uses the approved source, repository README/security policy, cutoff-effective public hosted terms/account/export pages, and post-cutoff hosted security claims only as labelled validation. No contract, procurement record, private control report, support case, hosted system, replacement candidate, or exit exercise was reviewed.

## Evidence Dimensions Used

Implementation, public responsibility statements, and public commercial terms are present. Negotiated commitments, observed service operation, internal vendor controls, cost, and replacement evidence are unknown.

## Current Source-Bounded Position

| Option | Control retained by library | External dependency | Evidence-supported continuity position | Decision dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Run | Infrastructure, versions, data stores, backups, upgrades, monitoring, accounts, capacity, support triage, and raw-data access | Upstream CE releases, latest-major/minor security fixes, community support, infrastructure/geolocation/mail/other providers | Maximum operational control and source visibility, but no support guarantee and no approved proof of the library's recovery/ownership practice. | [OI-001](../open-items.md#oi-001)–[OI-005](../open-items.md#oi-005), [OI-014](../open-items.md#oi-014), [OI-015](../open-items.md#oi-015) |

Cutoff-effective terms do not guarantee uninterrupted/error-free service, allow suspension/modification/discontinuance, and promise only reasonable-effort email support. | Obtain dated service-specific control evidence and negotiated acceptance for availability, recovery, support/escalation, termination, deletion, and export through [OI-015](../open-items.md#oi-015). |
| Replace | Requirements, procurement, governance, migration, and exit remain library-controlled | Unknown until candidates are selected | No candidate evidence was approved; continuity cannot be scored or treated as safer. | Use the same ownership, recovery, service-commitment, data-portability, deletion, and exit criteria in a future bounded selection. |

## Material Unknowns And Closure Routes

The public Terms of Service are evidence of the standard public boundary, not a procurement conclusion. Procurement should obtain applicable negotiated documents, service-support escalation, recovery objectives and proof, termination/export windows, subprocessor/change handling, and deletion/backup-expiry evidence. No legal conclusion is made.
