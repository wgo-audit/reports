## Material Unknowns And Access Limits

- The stated 18 properties, 2M visits/year, 14M pageviews/year, 25 staff, seasonal peaks, and new search/registration events are working assumptions. Monthly peak pageviews and billable custom-event volume must be measured from a controlled inventory before committing a hosted quota.
- The stated property/member scale is above the published standard Business limits. Obtain a dated written Enterprise configuration and quote that names pageview-plus-event quota, site/member limits, role/access needs, retention, API/reporting features, currency, taxes, discount treatment, billing cadence, renewal/cancellation, support/SLA, price-change notice, and data-export/exit terms.
- Determine the required journey method. Ordered funnel/user-journey features are a potential hosted entitlement dependency; do not treat the published plan card as proof that an acceptable Enterprise configuration includes it.
- Validate the library's actual current Run costs, infrastructure allocation, staff effort, contract authority, billing owner, payment/renewal control, retention/deletion requirements, and acceptable dashboard outage/event-loss window through the appropriate internal owners. They are unavailable in this read-only assessment.
- No current page can bind a procurement decision. Recheck the public table at procurement time and obtain formal vendor/procurement evidence; retain the price-table observation as post-cutoff validation only.
