# Technical Lead Notes

## Current Technical Position

The approved source is architecturally legible and has mature upstream change controls. A Phoenix/Elixir application and shared tracker surface feed process-local session and ingestion buffers; PostgreSQL holds application, configuration, membership, and Oban job state; ClickHouse holds analytical events and sessions; ordered cross-store migrations coordinate both authorities. The exact audited commit passed its enforced merge-group checks with 12,188 observed test-case passes and no observed failures/errors, but coverage was not measured, 67 dashboard E2E cases were EE-only, and no local product suite ran because dependencies were absent and restore was prohibited.

This is strong source evidence, not deployed Run proof. The library's version/digest, topology, ingress, identities, secrets, datastore versions/TLS, resources, queues, telemetry, backups, recovery, and enabled integrations are unknown. CE source also does not establish the hosted runtime, and no replacement source exists in scope.

## Safe Evolution Priorities

1. **Freeze identity before change.** Record the deployed tag/digest and redacted runtime boundary. Do not infer that `master`, a public tag, or a produced digest is the consumed artifact.
2. **Set acceptance semantics.** Obtain the event-loss/outage threshold, ordered-journey decision, and minimal event/data/access/retention contract before constructing tests or adding instrumentation.
3. **Use one representative synthetic path.** Exercise search and registration through tracker/API, ingestion, storage, goals, dashboards, CSV/API, monthly reporting, roles, negative edge cases, and accepted-versus-stored reconciliation.
4. **Treat upgrade as recovery.** Verify applicable green evidence, pre-change coordinated backups, pending interwoven migrations, irreversible boundaries, a dated restore, rollback stop conditions, and observability before CE change.
5. **Treat scale-out as analytics-schema behavior.** Prove session routing/transfer correctness before adding replicas; isolate or schedule reports, exports, deletion, and recovery around critical collection windows.
6. **Reduce diagnostic and alternate-access exposure.** Remove request/callback secrets from diagnostics, strengthen/throttle optional credentials, and disable unused shared/public/API paths.
7. **Exercise succession.** A named successor must reproduce the support boundary and complete the option-specific handoff within the approved recovery/service window; source history is not a current bus-factor result.
